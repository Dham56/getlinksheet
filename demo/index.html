LinkSheet Pro + HyperlinkRescue

Two companion Mac apps that turn a spreadsheet into clickable links to your files — and reconnect them when the files move. No Microsoft Excel required; everything runs on your Mac, and nothing is uploaded.

What they do

  • LinkSheet Pro — turns the entries in a spreadsheet into clickable links to your files. It matches the text in each cell — a contract number, a name, an invoice ID, whatever you use — to a file of the same name, and links them. Turn on Partial Match and it also links when the names aren't identical — a cell reading 35227 links to 35227-A15611.pdf — and if a cell could match more than one file, it flags that entry instead of guessing.
  • HyperlinkRescue — when those files later move or get reorganized and the links break, it reconnects every one to the right file, by name.

How they work together

LinkSheet creates the links. HyperlinkRescue keeps them alive when your files move. Anything you track in a spreadsheet — contracts, invoices, BOLs, deeds, part numbers — can be linked to its file and kept connected.
